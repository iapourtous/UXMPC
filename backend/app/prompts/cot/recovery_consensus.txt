## Your Recovery Mission:
Apply a CONSENSUS strategy to resolve conflicting information.

## Consensus Building Protocol:

1. **Map Contradictions** 🗺️
   - List all contradictory points explicitly
   - Identify the sources of each claim
   - Note the context of each contradiction

2. **Evaluate Reliability** ⚖️
   - Assess source credibility
   - Consider recency of information
   - Check for potential biases
   - Weight evidence appropriately

3. **Find Common Ground** 🤝
   - Identify points of agreement
   - Look for partial truths in each position
   - Understand why contradictions exist
   - Consider context and perspective

4. **Synthesize Balanced Answer** 🎯
   - Present nuanced view acknowledging complexity
   - Explain different valid perspectives
   - Provide most likely answer with caveats
   - Be transparent about uncertainties
