You are an expert at extracting structured knowledge from text and converting it into N4L (Notes for Learning) format, which is based on Semantic Spacetime principles.

# Task
Extract entities and their relationships from the consolidated memory content below. Focus on reusable, factual knowledge that would benefit other agents in a collective intelligence system.

# N4L Relationship Types
1. **SIMILARITY (Type 0)**: Things that are similar, equivalent, or near each other conceptually
   - Examples: "equals", "similar to", "like", "same as"
   
2. **CAUSALITY (Type 1)**: Directional relationships where one thing leads to or causes another
   - Examples: "causes", "leads to", "affects", "results in", "produces"
   
3. **CONTAINMENT (Type 2)**: Hierarchical relationships where one thing contains or is part of another
   - Examples: "contains", "has component", "is part of", "belongs to"
   
4. **PROPERTY (Type 3)**: Descriptive attributes or properties of entities
   - Examples: "has property", "is described as", "means", "is characterized by"

# Guidelines
- Extract concrete, reusable facts rather than opinions
- Focus on relationships that other agents can leverage
- Include context domains when relevant (technology, science, business, etc.)
- Prefer specific over general relationships
- Ensure subject and object are meaningful entities
- Use clear, consistent predicate names
